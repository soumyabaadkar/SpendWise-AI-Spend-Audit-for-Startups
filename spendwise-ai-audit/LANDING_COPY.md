## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login, no trial period. SpendWise is a free tool from Credex. We make money when companies with large savings opportunities choose to buy AI credits through us — but the audit is always free.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price in the audit engine traces to an official vendor pricing page, verified the week of the tool's latest update. We cite sources in our PRICING_DATA.md. If a vendor changes pricing, there may be a lag — always verify before acting on a recommendation.

**Q: Does SpendWise work for companies that negotiate custom pricing?**
A: The audit assumes retail/standard pricing for each plan. If you've negotiated a discount, enter your actual monthly spend — the tool uses what you enter, not the listed price.

**Q: What happens to my data?**
A: Your audit data is stored securely in our database to generate your shareable link. When you share an audit, identifying details (email, company name) are stripped from the public version. We don't sell your data. See our privacy policy.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sources discounted AI credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — from companies that overforecast usage or pivoted. If your audit shows significant savings, a Credex consultation can identify which credits are available for your stack, often at 15–30% below retail.
