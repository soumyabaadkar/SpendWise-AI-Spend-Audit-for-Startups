# ECONOMICS.md

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits at (estimated) 15–30% below retail. A startup spending $1,000/month on AI tools that switches $600/month of that through Credex credits saves $90–180/month. Credex's margin on the credit resale is roughly 10–20% of face value.

- **Revenue per converted customer:** $600 × 15% margin = **$90/month** (conservative)
- **Customer lifetime:** 12–24 months (credits are ongoing, not one-time)
- **LTV per customer:** $90 × 18 months = **$1,620**

## CAC by channel (from GTM.md)

| Channel | Cost | Est. leads generated | CAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Show HN post | $0 + 2 hours | 150 audits → 50 emails → 10 consultations → 3 conversions | $0 cash / ~$100 opportunity cost |
| Reddit posts | $0 + 2 hours | 80 audits → 25 emails → 5 consultations → 1–2 conversions | $0 cash |
| Slack cold DMs | $0 + 3 hours | 30 audits → 10 emails → 3 consultations → 1 conversion | $0 cash |
| Vendor partnership (Cursor newsletter) | Relationship cost | 2,000 audits → 600 emails → 120 consultations → 40 conversions | Minimal cash, high Credex leverage |
| Paid LinkedIn (future) | ~$200 CPL | 50 leads → 10 consultations → 2 conversions | $2,000 CAC — not worth it at this stage |

**Blended CAC at organic-only phase: ~$50–200** (time only)

## Conversion funnel

| Stage | Conversion rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor → Audit completed | 30–40% | High intent (they came for this) |
| Audit completed → Email captured | 30–45% | After value shown — below-average friction |
| Email captured → Consultation booked | 10–20% | Only for high-savings cases (>$500/mo) |
| Consultation → Credit purchase | 25–40% | Credex has a proven product; this is a sales conversion |

**End-to-end: 1,000 visitors → 350 audits → 140 emails → 20 consultations → 6 sales**

At $1,620 LTV: **$9,720 from 1,000 visitors.** Revenue per visitor: ~$9.70.

## What would have to be true for $1M ARR in 18 months

$1M ARR = $83,333/month revenue to Credex.

At $90/month per customer: **928 active customers** needed.

Working backwards from our funnel:
- Need ~155,000 qualified visitors over 18 months (~8,600/month)
- At 35% audit completion: ~54,000 audits
- At 40% email capture: ~21,600 leads
- At 15% consultation rate: ~3,240 consultations
- At 30% close rate: ~972 customers

**8,600 visitors/month is achievable via:**
- Show HN post: 5,000 visitors (month 1)
- Sustained Reddit/organic: 1,000–2,000/month
- Vendor partnership (Cursor et al): 3,000–5,000/month (month 3+)
- SEO on terms like "cursor vs copilot pricing", "ai tool cost comparison": 500–1,000/month (month 6+)

**What has to be true:**
1. At least one vendor partnership activates (Credex's existing relationships make this plausible)
2. The audit logic is defensible — finance-literate users share it and trust it
3. Credex's sales team converts consultations at ≥25%
4. Average customer spends $600/month through Credex (not all spend will migrate)

The math is tight but plausible. The biggest risk is consultation → close rate if Credex's sales capacity can't keep up with lead volume.
